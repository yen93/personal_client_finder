## How a travel & adventure company automated its sales & back-office — and saved ~40 hours a week

**The situation.** A growing travel & adventure company was running its sales and operations
largely by hand: prospecting, follow-ups, CRM updates, and reporting all ate into the team's week.
Leads went cold. Reports were assembled manually. Data was copy-pasted between tools.

**What I built (over 6 months).** A connected automation system that now runs itself every weekday:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **30+** | live automated workflows (scheduled, hands-free) |
| **30** | database functions + **18** scheduled data-sync jobs (the data backbone) |
| **10** | AI routines (drafting, analysis, triage) |
| **12** | live dashboards with real-time data |
| **~40 hrs/week** | estimated time saved across the team |

**Highlights of what's automated:**
- **Lead generation on autopilot** — prospecting that fills the pipeline every morning with zero
  manual research (~5 hrs/week saved on prospecting alone).
- **Never-cold follow-ups** — staged email sequences that nurture every lead automatically so nothing
  slips (multiple sequences, ~3–4 hrs/week each).
- **Self-updating CRM** — contacts, deals and accounts stay synced and clean with no manual exports;
  a note is logged at every follow-up stage.
- **Bounce & reply handling** — bounced addresses are auto-closed and replies routed to the sales
  team instantly, keeping the CRM accurate.
- **Zero-effort reporting** — a daily sales summary and weekly reports compile and send themselves.
- **Reliable scheduling** — ~30 jobs fire in the right order every weekday morning, self-healing and
  safe to retry.

**The result.** The team's repetitive office work — prospecting, follow-up, CRM upkeep, reporting —
now happens automatically and reliably, freeing an estimated ~40 hours a week for work only people
can do. The system is low-cost to run (mostly free-tier / near-zero compute) and proven in daily
production.
