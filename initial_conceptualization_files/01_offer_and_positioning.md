## 1. Positioning (one sentence)

**"I build automations that take repetitive office work off your team's plate — lead gen, email,
CRM, reporting — so you save hours every week without hiring."**

- Who you help: **small-to-mid businesses drowning in manual office work** (sales ops, admin,
  reporting, follow-ups). Industry-agnostic — you lead with the *outcome* (hours saved), not a niche.
- Why you're credible: you run a **30+ workflow automation system** for a travel/adventure company
  that saves it ~40 hrs/week (your anonymized case study — see `02_case_study_anonymized.md`).
- The wedge: most owners *know* they waste time on manual work but don't know what's automatable or
  where to start. Your paid **Audit** answers exactly that, cheaply, and becomes the on-ramp to builds.

### Positioning do's / don'ts
- **Do** sell outcomes ("save 10+ hrs/week," "never let a lead go cold") not tools ("I use n8n").
- **Do** anchor on the real system you run (scale = credibility).
- **Don't** list every technology — buyers don't care about Supabase vs n8n.
- **Don't** say "I can automate anything" on first touch — it reads as vague. Name 3–4 concrete wins.

## 2. The offer ladder (3 tiers)

A deliberate ladder: a cheap paid entry point that de-risks the first "yes," then build work, then
recurring revenue. **The cold email sells Tier 1 only** — never "hire me to build stuff."

### Tier 1 — Automation Audit  *(the lead offer / what cold email sells)*
- **What it is:** a fixed-price, fixed-scope diagnostic. You interview them (~45 min) + review how
  their team works, then deliver a short **Automation Roadmap**: the top 5–8 automatable processes,
  estimated hours saved each, difficulty, and a recommended build order with rough ROI.
- **Why it works:** low risk for them, paid (filters tyre-kickers), positions you as the expert, and
  the roadmap *is* the sales doc for Tier 2 — every line item is a potential build.
- **Deliverable:** 1 call + a 2–4 page roadmap PDF + a 15-min walkthrough.
- **Suggested price:** `‹set›` — **$500–$1,500 AUD**, fully **credited toward a build** if they
  proceed within 30 days (removes the "why pay for a quote" objection).
- **Turnaround:** ~1 week.

### Tier 2 — Done-for-you build  *(the core revenue)*
Fixed-scope builds, each mapped to something you've **already built** for MAG:
| Build | What it does | Proof you've built it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold-outreach machine | Find leads → verified emails → automated email drip → reply/bounce triage → CRM | This very project + MAG's outbound suite |
| CRM sync + live dashboard | Keep the CRM clean & synced, live sales/ops dashboard | AC caching, deals/contacts sync, 12 dashboards |
| Email triage & AI drafting | Auto-labels inbox, drafts replies in the owner's voice | email drafter + Gmail watchers |
| Reporting automation | Daily/weekly reports compiled & sent automatically | EOD/EOW report makers, sales daily email |
| Data-entry / sync jobs | Move data between tools on a schedule, no copy-paste | 18 scheduled sync jobs |

- **Pricing model:** fixed price per build (scoped from the Audit). **Suggested:** `‹set›` —
  **$2,000–$8,000 AUD** per build depending on scope. Avoid hourly — sell the outcome.
- **Turnaround:** 1–3 weeks per build.

### Tier 3 — Automation Partner (retainer)  *(the "high-paying client" goal)*
- **What it is:** monthly retainer — you keep their automations running (monitoring, fixes,
  deliverability, CRM hygiene) **and** ship 1–2 new small automations per month.
- **Why it matters:** this is the recurring revenue that turns one-off builds into *high-paying
  clients*. A handful of these = a real business.
- **Suggested price:** `‹set›` — **$800–$2,500 AUD/month** depending on surface area.

## 3. The "offer" in the cold email (what you actually pitch first)

Cold email does **not** pitch Tier 2/3. It pitches a tiny, specific, free-feeling first step:

> *A free 15-minute "time-waster audit" — I'll point out the 2–3 things your team is doing by hand
> that are easy to automate. No pitch, no obligation.*

That free mini-audit converts into the paid Tier 1 Audit, which converts into Tier 2 builds. Keep the
first ask **small and valuable**, not "let me sell you automation."

> Note: the cold email offers a **free** mini-audit as the opener (low friction to book a call); the
> **paid** Tier-1 Audit is what you sell *on* that call. Don't lead a cold stranger with a paid offer.

## 4. ICP (who to target first)

Start narrow to make the cold email specific, then widen. Good first segments (pick 1–2 — see open
decision #2 in the plan):
- **Sales-led SMBs** (agencies, real estate, B2B services, tour/travel operators like your case
  study) — they feel "leads going cold" pain acutely and value outbound automation.
- **Owner-operator businesses with an admin bottleneck** (clinics, trades, professional services) —
  reporting / scheduling / data-entry pain.
- **Businesses already using a CRM** (ActiveCampaign / HubSpot) — they've shown they'll pay for
  tooling and have data worth automating.

Target the **owner / GM / operations manager / office manager** — the person who feels the time drain
and can say yes. (This is the generalized version of the events-niche `TARGET_ROLES` list in
`insta_leads_enrichment`.)

## 5. Objection pre-answers (keep handy for replies)
- *"We're too small."* → "That's exactly who benefits most — you don't have staff to spare on manual
  work. The audit is free; worst case you get a free roadmap."
- *"We already use [tool]."* → "Great — I automate *between* your tools so they talk to each other."
- *"Is this AI that'll replace my team?"* → "No — it removes the boring repetitive parts so your team
  does the work only humans can."
- *"How do I know it works?"* → anonymized case study: a travel company, 30+ live automations,
  ~40 hrs/week saved.
